Adult Training for Troops- Introduction to Outdoor Leader Skills (IOLS) is an overnight training event that provides adult leaders the practical outdoor skills they need to lead and guide Scouts in the outdoors.

    2026 BALOO Training #8 (WIS). November 6 - 8, 2026 at Camp Wisdom. Hosted by Duck Creek District. Adult Training for Packs- Basic Adult Leader Outdoor Orientation (BALOO) is an overnight training event that introduces (or refreshes) adult Scouters and Leaders to the skills needed to plan and conduct Pack outdoor activities.

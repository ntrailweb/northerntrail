    Recruiting Doesn't End at School Night

    School Nights for Scouting are underway, and getting families through the door is only the first step. The follow-up afterward can make all the difference.

    ⏱️ 48 HOURS
    This week, I'm asking every pack to make sure every interested family gets a personal follow-up within 48 hours.

    A quick call, text, or email welcoming them and telling them exactly when and where to come next can turn interest into membership.

    🧭 Commissioners can help!

    Ask your Unit Commissioner for assistance following up with leads, identifying families who haven't completed registration, or simply making sure no one falls through the cracks.
    Recruit them. Welcome them. Get them Scouting.

Arapaho Chapter News

The Order of the Arrow is an honor camper service organization. Each district has a chapter and members are chosen by their peers.

Contact arapaho@miki.org with questions.

It's not too late to renew your OA membership go to: miki.org/event-registration/ and pay your dues.

The OA October ordeal is Oct 2-4. Register at miki.org. You have 18 months to experience your ordeal once you have been elected by your unit. Much friendship and fellowship awaits you on the other side.

2026 OA Fall Fellowship September 18 - 20, 2026 at Camp James Ray. This year's theme is Percy Jackson. Whether you're a seasoned hero or beginning your journey, this weekend will be filled with fellowship, games, competitions and memories.

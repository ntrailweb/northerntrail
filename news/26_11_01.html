      📋 Recharter of Scout Units
      There is a big change to recharter this year. Recharter for units with a 12/31 anniversary will open starting in October.
      ⚠️ There is NO grace period.
      If your unit does not renew by its charter anniversary date, the unit will lose its charter.
      If your unit renews on a date other than December 31, that date is your deadline. Missing the deadline can have significant consequences: even a long-established unit that restarts the process will become a 1st-year unit.
      Be prepared! It is best to complete your renewal by Thanksgiving. This gives your commissioner time to help resolve any issues before the deadline. Units waiting until the last week may not have enough time to address problems.
      Need help with recharter? Contact your unit commissioner.

      🧭 Commissioner Corner: The Unit Health Check
      How healthy is your unit? Sometimes four simple questions can tell you a lot. Take a moment to check in with your leadership team and see how your unit is doing.
      👥 1. Membership
      Are we growing or losing Scouts? Do we know why?
      🤝 2. Leadership
      Do we have enough engaged volunteers, or are a few people doing all the work?
      🏕️ 3. Program
      Are our Scouts excited about meetings and activities? Are we delivering the adventures they joined Scouting for?
      💙 4. Unit Culture
      Do Scouts and families feel welcomed and valued? Would a visiting family want to return?
      An honest conversation about these questions can highlight your unit's strengths and reveal opportunities for improvement.
      Your unit commissioner is here to help your unit thrive!

      📋 Recharter of Scout Units
      There is a big change to recharter this year. Recharter for units with a 12/31 anniversary will open starting in October.
      ⚠️ There is NO grace period.
      If your unit does not renew by its charter anniversary date, the unit will lose its charter.
      If your unit renews on a date other than December 31, that date is your deadline. Missing the deadline can have significant consequences: even a long-established unit that restarts the process will become a 1st-year unit.
      Be prepared! It is best to complete your renewal by Thanksgiving. This gives your commissioner time to help resolve any issues before the deadline. Units waiting until the last week may not have enough time to address problems.
      Need help with recharter? Contact your unit commissioner.

      From New Recruits to Lifelong Scouts

      Recruiting new Scouts is exciting, but getting them to join is only the first step. The real measure of success is whether they stay.

      The First Few Months Matter
      Are new Scouts making friends? Are they having fun? Do their families feel welcomed and included? Are they looking forward to the next meeting or adventure?
      ★ Your Challenge This Week ★
      Reach out to a family that joined this fall. Ask how things are going, what their Scout is enjoying, and whether there’s anything you can do to make their experience even better.

      A simple conversation can help a new family feel connected, supported, and excited about what comes next. Retention starts with making sure every Scout feels they belong.

      Need additional ideas or resources?
      Your Commissioner Corps is here to help!
